# Business Support — Monthly Program

## Purpose
Help identify business bottlenecks, risks, opportunities, and the next practical movement for a project or business process.

## Core chain
business request → current process state → bottleneck / risk → missing resource or decision → intervention → action → tracking

## Includes
- 2 personal sessions
- 4 weeks of support
- diagnosis of bottlenecks, risks, and opportunities
- practical action priorities
- optional symbolic/alchemical tools such as mandalas or artifacts when relevant to the agreed method
- progress review and next-step decision

## Client-facing result
Greater clarity, a prioritized next move, and supported momentum in the selected business process.

## Boundaries
This is strategic, symbolic, and coaching-style support. It does not guarantee revenue or replace legal, accounting, or investment advice.
