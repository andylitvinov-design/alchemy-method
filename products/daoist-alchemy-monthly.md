# Daoist Alchemy — Monthly Support

## Purpose
Support restoration of strength, health resource, stability, and Wu Xing balance through individually selected complementary tools.

## Core chain
current state → Dao resource level → Wu Xing bottleneck → individual support → weekly review → monthly level review

## Includes
- 2 personal sessions
- 4 weeks of support
- current resource and Wu Xing assessment
- individual homeopathy, naturopathy, Bach essence, oil, lifestyle, or practice recommendations as appropriate
- progress tracking
- end-of-cycle reassessment

## Client-facing result
A clearer understanding of current resource, an individual restoration plan, and supported movement toward greater stability.

## Boundaries
A monthly cycle is organized around movement toward the next level; advancement is not guaranteed and is assessed individually. This does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment.
