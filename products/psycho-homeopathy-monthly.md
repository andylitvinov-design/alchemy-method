# Psycho-Homeopathy / Alchemy of the Soul — Monthly Support

## Purpose
Work with stress subpersonalities and hidden emotional mechanisms that block goals, action, relationships, confidence, or self-expression.

## Core chain
request → current state → stress subpersonality / hidden mechanism → correction → remedy/support resonance → tracking → stabilization

## Includes
- 2 personal sessions
- 4 weeks of support
- initial diagnosis
- constellation, hypnotic, imagery, or psychotherapeutic correction as appropriate
- individually selected homeopathic or complementary support
- review of changes and support relevance
- end-of-cycle summary and next step

## Client-facing result
Greater clarity about what holds the problem in place, a supported shift in state, and a practical next step.

## Boundaries
No guaranteed cure or fixed result. This work does not replace medical or psychiatric care.
